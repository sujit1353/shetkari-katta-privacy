Privacy Policy

Shetkari Katta

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Shetkari Katta, developed and operated by Sujit Developers, handles information when users access or use the Shetkari Katta mobile application.

Shetkari Katta is an agricultural-focused application that provides features for farmers and shopkeepers, including agricultural information, shop information, agricultural product rates, photographs, and related services.

We are committed to handling user information responsibly and transparently.

3. Information We Collect

The information collected by Shetkari Katta depends on the features used by the user.

3.1 Account and authentication information

Shopkeeper accounts may require information necessary to create, authenticate, and maintain the account.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number(s)
  • Authentication credentials or authentication-related information
  • User/account identifier

3.2 Shopkeeper and shop information

Shopkeepers may provide information associated with their shop, including:

  • Shopkeeper name
  • Shop name
  • Village or location information provided by the user
  • Shopkeeper phone numbers
  • Shopkeeper profile information
  • Shop information

3.3 Photos and uploaded media

When a shopkeeper chooses to upload photographs, the selected images may include:

  • Shopkeeper profile photo
  • Shop photo
  • Other images intentionally uploaded through supported features

3.4 Agricultural information

Authorized shopkeepers may submit agricultural product rate information through the application.

3.5 Information submitted voluntarily

Information that a user voluntarily submits through the application's supported features may be processed to provide those features.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Shetkari Katta does not intentionally request or collect personal information that is not necessary for the application's supported functionality.

Users should not submit passwords, financial information, government identification documents, or other highly sensitive information unless a specific feature expressly requires it.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected or provided through the application may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing application functionality
  • Displaying shopkeeper and shop information
  • Displaying agricultural product rates
  • Displaying uploaded shopkeeper or shop photographs
  • Providing relevant application content
  • Maintaining application security
  • Preventing unauthorized use or abuse
  • Responding to user support requests
  • Processing account deletion requests
  • Maintaining and improving application reliability

6. Publicly Displayed Information

Some information submitted by authorized shopkeepers is intended to be displayed to other users as part of the application's functionality.

Depending on the shopkeeper profile and the information provided, this may include:

  • Shopkeeper name
  • Shop name
  • Village information
  • Shopkeeper phone numbers
  • Shopkeeper profile photo
  • Shop photo
  • Published agricultural product rates
Shopkeepers should only provide information that they are authorized to publish and should carefully consider information that they choose to make publicly visible.

7. Photos and Media Storage

When users upload photographs through supported features, the application transmits the selected media to its configured application media infrastructure.

Shopkeeper and shop photographs may be stored using the application's configured Cloudflare-based media infrastructure and associated application records.

Media may be associated with the relevant shopkeeper or shop and displayed to users when required by the application's functionality.

8. Backend, Database and Authentication Services

Shetkari Katta uses its configured backend infrastructure to provide authentication, account functionality, database operations, and application services.

The application's backend infrastructure may process account, profile, shop, agricultural rate, and other application data necessary to provide the requested features.

Backend access is controlled through authentication and appropriate access controls.

9. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

Shetkari Katta may use third-party service providers that provide infrastructure necessary for the application to operate.

Supabase

The application may use Supabase services for backend functionality, authentication, database services, and related application infrastructure.

Cloudflare

The application may use Cloudflare infrastructure for application media handling and storage of user-uploaded shopkeeper and shop photographs.

YouTube

Where the application displays videos or video-related information from the configured Shetkari Katta YouTube channel, the application may retrieve publicly available video information such as video titles and thumbnails through the configured YouTube integration.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

Third-party service providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide the application's functionality. Their handling of information is also subject to their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

10. Data Sharing

We may share or make information available only when necessary to provide the application's functionality, operate supporting infrastructure, comply with legal obligations, protect the application and its users, or respond to lawful requests.

Information intentionally published by a shopkeeper as part of the application's public shop or rate features may be visible to other application users.

We do not sell personal information to advertisers or third parties.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

Access to backend services and user information is controlled through appropriate authentication and access controls.

Information transmitted between the application and supported online services is protected using appropriate network security mechanisms, including HTTPS/TLS where supported.

However, no Internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's functionality, maintain account and security operations, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, or enforce applicable agreements.

Agricultural rate information and other application data may be retained or removed according to the application's current functionality and data-management practices.

When an account deletion request is completed, associated personal information and user-generated data will be deleted or otherwise handled according to the account deletion process, except where retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

13. Account Deletion

Users who create an account can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Account deletion is intended to permanently remove the account and associated user data that is eligible for deletion.

Request Account Deletion

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to upload photographs, shop information, phone numbers, and other content.

Users must not intentionally upload illegal, abusive, misleading, infringing, or unauthorized content.

15. Children's Privacy

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through the application without appropriate authorization, they may contact us at shetkarikatta10@gmail.com .

Third-party web services or web content accessed through the application may use their own technologies according to their respective privacy practices.
