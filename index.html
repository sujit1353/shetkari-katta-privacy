Privacy Policy

Shetkari Katta

1. Introduction

Welcome to Shetkari Katta. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and handled when you use the Shetkari Katta mobile application.

Shetkari Katta is an agricultural-focused application that provides features for farmers and shopkeepers, including agricultural market/rate information and related services.

By using the application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how you use the application and which features you access.

Information provided by users

Depending on the account type and features used, the application may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Phone number(s)
  • Village or location-related information provided by the user
  • Shop name
  • Shopkeeper profile information
  • Shop information
  • Photos uploaded by shopkeepers
  • Account authentication information
  • Other information voluntarily provided through the application

Account information

When an account is created, information necessary to authenticate and maintain the account may be processed by the application's backend services.

User-uploaded photos

If a shopkeeper chooses to upload a profile photo or shop photo, the selected image may be uploaded to the application's media storage system and associated with the relevant shopkeeper or shop record.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through the application may be used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Display relevant profile and shop information
  • Display information submitted by authorized shopkeepers
  • Display agricultural rates and related information
  • Provide application functionality
  • Maintain and improve the application's reliability and security
  • Respond to support or account-related requests
  • Prevent unauthorized access or misuse of the application

4. Shopkeeper Information

Shopkeepers may provide information such as their name, shop name, village, phone numbers, profile photo, shop photo, and other information required for their shop profile.

Some shopkeeper information may be displayed to other users when required for the functionality of the application.

Users should only provide information that they are authorized to share and should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information.

5. Agricultural Rate Information

Authorized shopkeepers may submit agricultural product rate information through the application.

Information submitted through these features may be displayed to other users as part of the application's functionality.

6. Photos and Media

When a user chooses to upload a photo, the application may transmit the selected image to the application's configured media storage infrastructure.

Uploaded photos may be associated with the relevant user, shopkeeper, or shop record and may be displayed within the application where required by its functionality.

Users should not upload photographs containing information that they do not have permission to share.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party infrastructure and services to provide its functionality.

Depending on the features actually enabled in the released application, these services may include backend, authentication, database, media storage, content delivery, or video-related services.

Information processed by such services is handled according to the applicable service providers' policies and the configuration used by Shetkari Katta.

The application does not intentionally sell users' personal information to third parties.

8. Data Storage and Security

User information and application data may be stored using the application's configured backend and storage services.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain legitimate business or security requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

When an account is successfully deleted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized according to the application's account-deletion process, except where retention is required or permitted by law.

10. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Shetkari Katta account and associated personal information through the account-deletion mechanism provided by the application and its associated web resource.

Account deletion may result in the permanent removal of the user's account and associated user-generated information.

Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or satisfy legitimate security requirements.

Account deletion instructions and the external deletion resource are provided separately by Shetkari Katta.

11. Children's Privacy

Shetkari Katta is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

12. Your Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring that the information they provide is accurate and that they have the necessary rights or permissions to provide information and upload photographs.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, data practices, legal requirements, or services used by Shetkari Katta.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective or last-updated date.
