Request Account Deletion

What to Include in Your Request

To help us identify the correct account, please send the request from the email address associated with your account, if applicable, and provide enough information for us to identify your Shetkari Katta account.

Do not send your password, authentication codes, or other sensitive credentials in the deletion request.

What Happens After a Deletion Request

After receiving a valid account deletion request, we will verify the request as reasonably necessary to protect the account owner and prevent unauthorized deletion.

Once the request is verified and processed, the applicable account and associated personal information will be deleted according to the Shetkari Katta account-deletion process.

Users may be signed out of the application and will no longer be able to use the deleted account.

Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the account type and information associated with the account, deletion may include eligible information such as:

  • Account information
  • Profile information
  • Shopkeeper information
  • Shop information
  • Phone numbers associated with the account
  • Uploaded profile or shop photographs
  • User-generated application data associated with the account

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, for legitimate security purposes, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or other legitimate purposes.

Any retained information will be handled according to applicable legal requirements and the Shetkari Katta Privacy Policy.
